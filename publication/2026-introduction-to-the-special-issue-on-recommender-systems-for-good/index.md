It introduces contributions addressing the emerging RS4Good agenda, including normative risks, vulnerable and high-stakes contexts, and constructive applications in areas such as healthcare, education, tourism, and sustainability. The issue emphasizes that recommender systems for good are defined not only by application domain but also by the values, design processes, and evaluation criteria that guide their development."
